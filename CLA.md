## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot will ask you to comment exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the date and the pull request in the `cla-signatures`
branch. If you contribute on behalf of an entity, also state the entity's legal name in a comment
and confirm that you are authorized to sign Part B for it.
