# Not legal advice

This software models laws and regulations (for example the California CARS Act (SB 766), the FTC
Used Car Rule, California Vehicle Code and DMV record rules, and the FTC Safeguards Rule) to help
dealers follow them. **It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a lawyer.**

- Rules, fees, deadlines and document text in this repository come from cited, dated sources in
  `docs/research/`. Laws change, and sources can be wrong or incomplete.
- Generated documents and forms are templates. A qualified attorney should review them before you
  use them with customers.
- You are responsible for your dealership's compliance. Check the current law in your jurisdiction.
- The project is in early development and is **not ready for real deals or real customer data**.

If you find a rule that is wrong or out of date, please open an issue or pull request with a
citation to the official source.
